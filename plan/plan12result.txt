5. Important design decisions
- Uniform filter graph for N=1 and N>1 (no special cases); amix normalize=0
  preserves voice levels (documented clipping risk on overlaps).
- apad before amix guarantees full-duration output even in edge cases; atrim
  enforces exact video duration.
- State writes happen only on the Tkinter main thread; worker returns data.
- Wavs may extend past their subtitle end (no stretch); composition trims at
  video duration; a subtitle starting at/after video end is a hard error.
